The application is a one-directory bundle. Qt shared libraries live in
_internal/PySide6/Qt/lib/ and are dynamically loaded. They are not statically
linked into Yarnelo. You may replace those LGPL components with compatible
modified versions in your own copy, subject to their respective licenses.
Repacking an AppImage is unnecessary for this: use --appimage-extract and run
the resulting squashfs-root/AppRun after replacing compatible libraries.

identify upstream source repositories; corresponding release tags follow those
recorded versions. This notice does not grant a license to Yarnelo itself.
